Privacy and security in Glasstual

What’s stored where

  • Passwords and keys: Server, account, proxy and OAuth passwords, and SASL signing keys, are stored in your keychain.
  • Settings and servers: Stored in Glasstual’s own container on your Mac.
  • History: Messages Glasstual keeps for restoring and searching conversations stay in its container on your Mac.
  • Transcript logs: Written only if you turn them on, to the folder you choose in Advanced settings.

Export and import settings

Choose File > Export Settings to save your settings and servers to a file. Keychain passwords and certificate references are never included. Connect commands are included only if you select “Include connect commands,” because they may hold passwords.

Choose File > Import Settings to preview a file before applying it. Glasstual points out changes that send commands to servers or change what it trusts, saves a recovery backup first, and doesn’t connect imported servers on its own.

Images and links

  • Inline images load only over HTTP and HTTPS, with limits on size. To turn them off, open Glasstual > Settings, click Style, then deselect “Show inline images.” Each channel can also turn them off in Channel Properties.
  • A server that uses a proxy loads images only through it. See Connect through a proxy or Tor.
  • Before a link opens in another app, Glasstual asks.

What others can see

  • Files and direct chats reveal your IP address to the other person. See Send and receive files.
  • Glasstual can answer client-to-client (CTCP) requests such as VERSION. Change this in Glasstual > Settings > Connection.
  • Typing notifications are shared only if you select “Share when I am typing” in IRCv3 settings.

Spotlight and Shortcuts

Spotlight gets only the names of servers and channels, never messages; direct conversations are left out unless you include them. Shortcuts actions ask you to confirm a message before it’s sent. See Use Spotlight, Shortcuts and Focus.
