Join channels and start conversations

Join a channel

  • Choose Channel > Add Channel, enter the channel name, then click Save. To join it, select it in the sidebar and choose Channel > Join Channel.
  • Or type /join #channel in the message field.
  • To browse the network’s channels, choose Server > Channel List.

Channels in a server’s channel list can join automatically when you connect. To change that, select the channel, then choose Channel > Channel Properties.

Leave or remove a channel

Choose Channel > Leave Channel to part it but keep it in the sidebar. Choose Channel > Delete Channel to remove it.

Start a private conversation

  • Control-click a member, then choose Send Message.
  • Or type /query nickname, or /msg nickname message to send one message.

To close it, choose Conversation > Close Conversation.

Change a channel’s topic and modes

If you have permission, choose Channel > Modify Topic, or use Channel > Modes. The Channel menu also lists the channel’s bans, ban exceptions, invite exceptions and quiets.

Highlights and people

  • Glasstual highlights messages that mention your nickname. To add other words, open Glasstual > Settings, then click Highlights. Choose Window > Highlights to see recent highlights.
  • Choose Window > People to ignore someone or to be told when they come online.
  • To silence one conversation, turn off its notifications in Server Properties or Channel Properties.
