Find your way around the main window

The main window has a sidebar of servers and conversations, the transcript of the selected conversation with the message field below it, and the member list of the selected channel.

Sidebar

Each server lists its channels and private conversations. Badges count unread messages, and a highlighted badge means someone mentioned you. Networks of a soju bouncer appear under the bouncer’s account; see Use a bouncer.

  • To find a conversation by name, choose Go > Filter Sidebar, or choose Go > Channel Search to search every server.
  • To go to the next unread or active channel, use the commands in Go > Channels.
  • To keep a conversation at the top, select it, then choose Channel > Pin to Favorites or Conversation > Pin to Favorites.
  • To hide or show the sidebar, choose View > Hide Sidebar.

Transcript

Messages appear as lines or as bubbles. To choose, open Glasstual > Settings, click Style, then choose a layout. The default colors follow your Mac’s appearance, accent color and Increase Contrast setting.

To use other colors, choose a theme preset, such as Classic, GitHub or Solarized, or set each color yourself. Classic uses Glasstual’s own colors in place of the system’s. To change the transcript font, click Change beside Font, then choose a font in the Fonts panel.

  • Joins, parts and similar events are collapsed into a single line. Click it to show them.
  • To find text in the conversation, choose Edit > Find > Find.
  • To mark where you stopped reading, choose View > Mark Scrollback. Choose View > Mark All as Read to clear every unread badge.
  • To jump back to the newest messages, choose Go > Jump to Present.
  • Control-click a message, link, channel name or nickname for more commands. The menu lists only the commands that apply.

Message field

  • Press Return to send. Press Command-Return to send the message as an action, like /me.
  • Press Tab to complete nicknames, channels and commands. Type a slash to see the commands that match what you’ve typed.
  • Press Up Arrow and Down Arrow to go through messages you’ve sent.
  • Use the Format menu for bold, italic, underline and colors.

Member list

The member list shows who is in the selected channel, sorted by rank. To hide or show it, choose View > Hide Member List.

Control-click a member to get information about them, start a private conversation, change their rank, ban or kick them, mute or ignore them, or change the color of their nickname. Sending a file is under Client-to-Client. To choose what double-clicking a member does, open Glasstual > Settings, then click Controls.
