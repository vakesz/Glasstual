Use Spotlight, Shortcuts and Focus

Find conversations in Spotlight

Open Glasstual > Settings, click General, then select “Show servers and channels in Spotlight.” Only names are indexed; messages are never added. Select “Include direct messages” to add private conversations too. Turning the setting off removes everything Glasstual added.

Use Shortcuts

Glasstual offers these actions in the Shortcuts app and to Siri:

  • Send IRC Message: Sends a message to a channel or person after you confirm it. Text that starts with a slash is sent as it is, never run as a command.
  • Join IRC Channel: Joins a channel on a connected or connecting server.
  • Connect to IRC Server and Disconnect from IRC Server.
  • Open IRC Conversation: Opens a channel or direct conversation.

Quiet Glasstual with a Focus

  1. Open System Settings, click Focus, then choose a Focus.
  2. Add a Focus filter and choose Glasstual.
  3. Choose whether the Focus mutes notifications, sets you away, or both. You can limit it to some networks or to channels or direct messages, keep notifications for highlights and direct messages, and enter an away message.

To be marked away when your Mac is idle, open Glasstual > Settings, click General, then select “Set away status when your Mac is idle.”
