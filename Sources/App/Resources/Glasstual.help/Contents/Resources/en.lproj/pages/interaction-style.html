Choose how Glasstual asks you questions

Glasstual can ask you things in dialogs, or right in the conversation, where you answer by clicking or typing. One setting covers both questions and editors.

Choose a style

Open Glasstual > Settings, click General, then under Questions and Editors choose As Dialogs or In the Conversation. The Welcome window also asks, after Look and Feel. A change applies to the next question; questions already showing stay where they are.

Answer a question in the conversation

A question appears as a card above the message field, in the conversation it’s about or in the server’s console. If questions are waiting in other conversations, a line above the message field says so, with a Show button. Click one of a card’s buttons, or type a command in the message field:

  • /answer followed by the question’s number and your answer, for example /answer 2 send. The answer can be its word or the title of its button.
  • /yes for the one way forward, or /no to decline. With several questions waiting, add the number, for example /no 3.
  • /prompts to list the questions waiting for an answer.
  • Where a question offers “Don’t ask again,” add --remember to /answer or /yes.

Only what you type in the message field answers a question; scripts, timers and connect commands can’t. An answered question leaves no line in the conversation or in your logs. Messages that only tell you something appear as notices; sending a message dismisses them.

Questions that need care

Some questions could cost you if answered by accident: sending your password to a server whose certificate wasn’t checked, trusting a direct-connection certificate, accepting a direct chat, sending files you dropped, and opening a link in another app. Each card shows the command that answers it. Type the answer’s word, such as /answer 2 send; /yes, a number alone and the Return key never answer these questions, and a click counts only once the card has settled on screen. /no always declines.

When a password or token is needed, the card has its own secure field. A secret is never typed as part of a command, and is never kept in the conversation, your logs or input history. Switching conversations clears a half-typed secret.

Editors as commands

Editors such as Modify Topic, Change Nickname and Server Properties open as a ready-to-edit command in the message field, for example /topic #channel followed by the current topic. Press Return to run it. Server and channel settings have their own commands, /serverconfig and /channelconfig; type list after either to see every setting.

What stays a window

  • Deciding whether to trust a server’s certificate always uses the certificate panel.
  • Lists such as the channel list and ban lists stay windows.
  • Questions raised inside an open sheet or in Settings are answered there.
  • Choosing a client certificate or a file uses the system panels.
