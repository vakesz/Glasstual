Choose how Glasstual asks you questions

Glasstual can ask you things in dialogs, or right in the conversation, where you answer by clicking or typing. One setting covers both questions and editors.

Choose a style

Open Glasstual > Settings, click General, then under Questions and Editors choose As Dialogs or In the Conversation. The Welcome window also asks, after Look and Feel. A change applies to the next question; questions already showing stay where they are.

Answer a question in the conversation

A question appears as a card in the conversation it’s about, or in the server’s console. Click one of its buttons, or type a command in the message field:

  • /answer followed by the question’s number and your answer, for example /answer 2 send.
  • /yes for the usual answer, or /no to decline.
  • /prompts to list the questions waiting for an answer.

An answered question leaves no line in the conversation or in your logs. Messages that only tell you something appear as notices.

Questions that need care

Some questions could cost you if answered by accident: sending your password to a server whose certificate wasn’t checked, accepting a direct chat, sending files you dropped, and opening a link in another app. For these, type the answer’s word, such as send. /yes, a number alone and the Return key never answer them.

When a password is needed, Glasstual switches the message field to a secure field. A password is never typed as part of a command and is never kept in history.

What stays a window

  • Deciding whether to trust a server’s certificate always uses the certificate panel.
  • Lists such as the channel list and ban lists stay windows.
  • Editors such as Modify Topic and Change Nickname open as a ready-to-edit command in the message field, for example /topic #channel followed by the current topic.
