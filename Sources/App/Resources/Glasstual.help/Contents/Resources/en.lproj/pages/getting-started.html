Get started with Glasstual

Glasstual is an IRC client for Mac. The first time you open it, the Welcome window walks you through a few choices. You can change all of them later in Settings.

Set up Glasstual

  1. Enter the nickname others will see. A real name and an alternate nickname, used when your nickname is already taken, are optional.
  2. Under Look and Feel, choose an appearance (System, Light or Dark), a chat style (Lines or Bubbles) and a text size.
  3. Under How Glasstual Asks, choose whether questions open as dialogs or appear in the conversation. See Choose how Glasstual asks you questions.
  4. Under Notifications, choose whether Glasstual notifies you about mentions and private messages, and whether it plays sounds. When you continue, macOS asks whether Glasstual may show notifications.
  5. Under Your First Network, choose a network from the list or enter a server address and port. Turn on Use SSL/TLS to encrypt the connection. If you have an account on the network, turn on Sign in with SASL and enter the account name and password.
  6. Choose any suggested channels you want to join, then review your choices and click Finish. If Connect when finished is selected, Glasstual connects right away.

To skip setup, click Set Up Later. You can add a network at any time; see Add a server.

Open the Welcome window again

Choose Help > Welcome to Glasstual.

Set the identity for new servers

Choose Glasstual > Settings, click Identity, then enter the nickname, username, real name and away nickname that new servers start with. Every field is optional.
